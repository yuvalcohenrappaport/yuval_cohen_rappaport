Café Loyalty & BI Platform

An end-to-end loyalty, competitions, and business-intelligence platform built for an independent Tel Aviv café — running on the floor every day.

Problem

Independent cafés run loyalty on punch cards and gut feel. The owner wanted regulars coming back more often — and real visibility into what's working — without enterprise POS lock-in.

WhatsApp AI Assistant

WhatsApp was eating hours a day — events buried in chats, commitments forgotten. So I built an assistant that watches my threads, drafts replies in my voice, and quietly syncs the rest to Calendar and Tasks.

● Deployed at home/IoT/2025

ESP32 Coffee-Machine Tank Sensor

My espresso machine kept dry-firing the boiler when the tank ran empty mid-shot. A capacitive sensor taped to the reservoir, an ESP32, and Telegram pings me the second it hits empty.

Insight

You don't need a level gauge. You need to know the moment the tank hits empty — with enough debouncing to ignore sloshing while pulling shots.

What I shipped
  • Non-contact capacitive sensor on the reservoir wall — no plumbing changes.
  • Telegram alerts with interactive commands and a configurable heartbeat.
  • 5-minute polling, 1-minute debounce, WiFi auto-reconnect for unattended runtime.
Success metric

Empty state detected within one check cycle. Zero false alarms over a 30-day window. Heartbeat confirms the device is alive.
